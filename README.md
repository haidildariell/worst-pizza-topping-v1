## The entry

> Our pizza comes topped with wet cardboard — it folds like a love letter, and the box it arrived in is technically the second topping.

## Why wet cardboard

The joke lands in two beats. First, the topping itself: wet cardboard, the least appetising thing a pizza could carry. Second, the twist: the box it arrived in counts as a topping too — so the pizza was never really topped once, it was topped twice, and both times by packaging.

## How it works

Run `python3 main.py` and the line above is written to `/output/entry.txt`, exactly as-is, with no trailing newline.
